A few things to know.

Before you bring your own project.

What makes the canvas a development environment?

You can shape the view you need, plan new work, launch agents, and review changes in one workspace. Diagrams, code, and tasks become parts of the same development process.

What can I try on this page?

The embedded canvas uses the actual product interface with a prepared Aurora AI example. Move blocks, explore the canvas, and open code details. The recordings show more of the installed application.

Can I launch an AI agent in this demo?

Agent execution is available in the installed app. This browser demo does not start AI agents or modify a real repository.

Where does my code run?

CodeChroma runs locally. You choose your AI provider; what is sent to that provider depends on your tools and configuration.

Is CodeChroma ready for production?

CodeChroma is in pre-alpha. Expect rough edges and incomplete features as the product develops.
